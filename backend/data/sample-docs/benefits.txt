# Brightpath Employee Benefits

This guide explains the benefits Brightpath offers on top of salary. It applies to all full-time employees and, where stated, to part-time employees. Questions about benefits should be sent to benefits@brightpath.example.

# Health Insurance

All full-time employees are covered by the Brightpath group health insurance plan from their first day of work. There is no waiting period and no medical check is needed to join. Part-time employees who work at least 3 days per week are also covered. The insurance provider is Gulf Care, and every employee receives a digital insurance card in the Gulf Care app during their first week.

The plan covers doctor visits, specialist visits, hospital stays, emergency treatment, laboratory tests and prescribed medicine. Doctor and specialist visits have a co-payment of 2 BHD per visit at network clinics. Hospital stays and emergency treatment have no co-payment. Prescribed medicine is covered at 80%, and the employee pays the remaining 20% at the pharmacy.

Employees can add their husband or wife and up to 3 children under the age of 21 to the plan. Brightpath pays 100% of the employee's own insurance and 50% of the cost for each family member. Family members must be added within 30 days of joining Brightpath, or within 30 days of a marriage or birth. After that, they can only be added during the yearly renewal in January.

Dental treatment is covered up to 150 BHD per person per year. This includes check-ups, cleaning, fillings and extractions. Cosmetic treatment such as teeth whitening and braces for adults is not covered. Eye tests are covered once per year, and glasses or contact lenses are covered up to 60 BHD per year.

When you visit a network clinic, show your digital insurance card and the clinic will bill Gulf Care directly. If you visit a clinic outside the network, you pay first and then submit a claim in the Gulf Care app with the receipt and the medical report. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the visit. Gulf Care pays approved claims to your bank account within 10 working days, at the network rate.

Treatment outside Bahrain is covered only in an emergency, and only for up to 30 days per trip. For planned treatment abroad, you need written approval from Gulf Care before travelling.

# Pension

Brightpath adds 5% of your basic salary to a company pension savings plan every month. If you also save part of your salary in the plan, Brightpath matches it up to an extra 3%, so the most Brightpath can add is 8%. You can change how much you save once every 3 months in the HR portal. The company part becomes yours after 2 years of service.

# Wellness Allowance

Every employee receives a wellness allowance of 20 BHD per month. It can be used for a gym membership, sports classes or a fitness app subscription. Submit the receipt in the HR portal before the 25th of the month, and the amount is paid with that month's salary. Unused allowance does not roll over to the next month.

# Employee Discount

Employees get a free Brightpath Business account for personal use, and 50% off Brightpath plans for up to 4 family members. Ask the support team to apply the discount to a family member's account.
