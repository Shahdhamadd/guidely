# Brightpath Expenses and Travel Policy

This policy explains what Brightpath pays for and how to get reimbursed.

# Submitting Expenses

Submit expenses in the Expensify app within 30 days of the purchase. Every expense needs a photo of the receipt. Expenses above 50 BHD need approval from your manager before you spend the money. Approved expenses are paid with your next monthly salary.

# Meals

When travelling for work, the daily meal allowance is 25 BHD inside Bahrain and 60 BHD abroad. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Team lunches need manager approval and are limited to 10 BHD per person.

# Travel

Flights must be booked through the company travel portal at least 14 days before the trip. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Hotels are limited to 80 BHD per night.

# Equipment and Home Office

Each employee receives a one-time home office budget of 150 BHD for items like a chair, monitor, or headset. Employees can also claim up to 10 BHD per month for their home internet if they work remotely at least one day a week.

# Training and Learning

Each employee has a yearly learning budget of 300 BHD for courses, books, and conferences. Training requests are approved by your manager. If you leave Brightpath within 6 months of a course that cost more than 150 BHD, you must repay the cost.
