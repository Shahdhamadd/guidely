# Brightpath Employee Policy

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Brightpath. Questions about this policy should be sent to hr@brightpath.example.

# Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 to 17:00, Sunday to Thursday. Employees may start any time between 7:30 and 10:00 as long as they work 8 hours per day. Core hours, when everyone must be available for meetings, are 10:00 to 15:00.

# Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 21 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their working days. Employees with more than 5 years of service receive 25 days per year.

Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance. Requests for more than 10 consecutive days need approval from both your manager and the HR department.

Up to 5 unused leave days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March, otherwise they are lost.

# Sick Leave

Employees receive up to 15 days of paid sick leave per year. If you are sick for more than 2 consecutive days, you must upload a medical certificate to the HR portal. Tell your manager before 10:00 on any day you are sick.

# Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week after completing their 3-month probation period. Remote days must be agreed with your manager in advance and recorded in the team calendar. Employees must be reachable on Slack during core hours when working remotely.

Working from another country requires written approval from HR and is limited to 20 days per year.

# Parental Leave

Mothers receive 60 days of fully paid maternity leave. Fathers receive 5 days of fully paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 3 months of the birth.
